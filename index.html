03 / How it works

From idea to launch.

A process that does not waste your time: scope and price agreed in writing, then steady progress until handover.

Discuss

Tell us the service, the game and the goal. We ask the questions that matter and reply within 24 hours on business days.

  • Free scoping conversation, no obligation
  • Discord or email, whichever you prefer
  • Straight answers about feasibility and timeline

Plan

Scope, price and timeline are agreed in writing before anything starts — so there are no surprises at the end.

  • Fixed scope with a clear deliverable list
  • Milestones and dates you can plan around
  • One point of contact the whole way

Design

Concepts are built around your actual content, not a template with your logo dropped in.

  • Thumbnails and artwork tested against the mobile feed
  • Gamemode and community layouts shaped around players
  • Work-in-progress previews as things take shape

Build

Servers, plugins and communities are configured, tested and documented — on the timeline you were given.

  • Paper & Spigot plugins written from scratch
  • Everything tested before it reaches your players
  • Progress updates without you having to ask

Deliver

Handover with files, docs and support. You own everything we make — sources, project files and configurations included.

  • Full handover with documentation
  • You own everything — no lock-in
  • Support after launch when you need it

FAQ

Questions, answered.

01 What does KBJS Studios actually do?

Four things: Minecraft server development and custom Paper/Spigot plugins, Discord server setup and community builds, gaming YouTube thumbnails, and YouTube Shorts editing. Most clients start with one service and come back for more.

02 How fast do you reply?

Within 24 hours on business days, on Discord or by email. Tell us the service, the game and the goal and you will get scope and pricing back in the same conversation.

03 Who owns the finished work?

You do. Handover includes plugin sources, design files, configurations and documentation. Nothing is locked behind us — everything we build for you is built to be handed over.

04 Do you only work on Minecraft?

Minecraft is the speciality, but thumbnails and short-form editing cover Valorant, Fortnite, Roblox and more. If your project touches the technical side of a gaming channel, it is probably a fit.

05 How does pricing work?

Scope and price are agreed in writing before anything starts. You will know exactly what is being delivered and what it costs — no hourly surprises, no scope creep.

06 How do I start a project?

Join the Discord or send an email. Tell us the service, the game and the goal — you will get scope and pricing back within 24 hours on business days.
