Choosing

Which game tests what?

Aim Trainer measures click speed, Target Rush adds combo pressure, Reaction Shot isolates reflexes, Precision Range punishes sloppy mouse control, and Tracking Trial measures focus on a moving target — the skill that matters most in tactical shooters. Play two or three a week and the difference shows within a month.

FAQ

Mini game FAQs

Are these mini games really free?▾

Yes — completely free, with no sign-up. They run entirely in your browser.

Do I need to install anything?▾

No. Open the page and play; everything runs client-side on canvas.

How are scores saved?▾

Your personal best is stored on your device and submitted to a global leaderboard when you save a name.

Do they work on mobile?▾

They work on touch devices, though a mouse gives more accurate results in the precision tests.
