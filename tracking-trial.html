About this test

How Tracking works

Tracking Trial measures the ability to keep your crosshair on a moving target without losing it — the core skill behind following a strafing opponent. The target is driven by physics, so its path stays learnable, and the test is about how long you hold focus before losing it.

Your tracking percentage updates live and grades run from S (95%+) down to D. This is the test most directly tied to third-person and tactical shooters, where whole fights are spent tracking rather than flicking.

What it measures

Keep your cursor on the moving target for 20 seconds. Graded A–D so you can compare runs over time.

How to improve

  • Start the movement and follow with your eyes before your hand.
  • Use small, smooth corrections instead of large swings.
  • Keep your elbow anchored to the desk for stability.
  • Accept a lower percentage first — consistency is the goal.

FAQ

Tracking FAQs

What is a good Tracking Trial score?▾

85%+ earns an A. 95%+ is elite control.

Why do I lose the target immediately?▾

You are reacting to movement instead of anticipating it. Look slightly ahead.

Does tracking help in shooters?▾

Yes — tracking is the primary aim skill in third-person and tactical shooters.

Is it free?▾

Yes, free in your browser with no sign-up.

How long is the test?▾

20 seconds, with target speed increasing the longer you hold on.
