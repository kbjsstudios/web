Azure Aura
==========

Notes:
This set is based off of the Cool Outer Glow that I did 18 years ago. OMG I am old.  Which isn't much, since it was just an outer glow affect done in photoshop and some highlights off of the original design (I do not remember who did the original). 

I was going to name it "cool blue outer glow 4.0", but i decided on Azure Aura instead.  I went with the Red Link color because that's what the classic site of Cursors-4U.com did.  This cursor was very popular back in the days.  And now, for your enjoyment I have created a modern version, in a complete set.  

Crisp cyan outlines, dark teal borders, and softly beveled surfaces give each cursor a polished, dimensional finish. The animations bring distinct movement to everyday actions: a traveling light traces the working pointer, a glowing comet orbits the busy cursor, and a glass reflection sweeps diagonally across alternate select.   One extra busy cursor, that echo's infinitely into the universe.

Hope you like

Installation:
  1. Right-click install.inf
  2. Select 'Install'
  3. Open Control Panel > Mouse > Pointers
  4. Select 'Azure Aura' from the Scheme dropdown
