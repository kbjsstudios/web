About this test

How Aim works

The Aim Trainer measures the two things that decide a shooter match: how fast you acquire a target and how accurately you click it. Targets spawn in random positions and every miss is counted, so you cannot inflate a score by spraying.

Your report shows total hits plus accuracy and a grade — Sharpshooter at 25+ hits, Good aim at 15+, Not bad at 8+, otherwise Keep practicing. Aim at the centre of each target and let your flicks, not your clicks, do the work.

What it measures

Flick, click and track. Hit as many targets as you can before the clock runs out. Graded A–D so you can compare runs over time.

How to improve

  • Move your eyes to the target before your hand arrives.
  • Keep the mouse near the next spawn area instead of parked in a corner.
  • Short daily sessions beat one long session — aim degrades with fatigue.
  • Train both hands if you play with keyboard and mouse.

FAQ

Aim FAQs

What counts as a good Aim Trainer score?▾

25+ hits in 30 seconds with high accuracy is strong. Consistency matters more than one high score.

Why is my accuracy low?▾

You are probably clicking before the target fully renders. Wait for it to appear, then flick and click in one motion.

Is Aim Trainer free?▾

Yes — free, no download and no account needed.

Does it help in FPS games?▾

Yes. Click speed and target acquisition transfer directly to Valorant, Fortnite and CS.

How often should I use it?▾

Ten minutes a day, most days. Aim is a trainable skill.
